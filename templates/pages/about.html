About Book ReSeller

Book ReSeller connects readers who want to buy and sell used and rare books locally.

How it works

  1. Browse available books and find nearby sellers.
  2. Message the seller and agree on a price and pickup.
  3. Meet, inspect the book, and pay the seller directly.

Sellers manage their own listings. The marketplace supports conversations, pickup agreements, and reporting; it does not collect payment or arrange courier delivery.
