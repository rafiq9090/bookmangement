Privacy

Account and marketplace data

The marketplace stores account details, listings, messages, pickup agreements, reviews, and reports to support buying, selling, and moderation. Passwords are stored as hashes.

Location

Location access is requested through location controls. Recommendation coordinates are rounded before being saved to the browser session. Listing pickup areas and map coordinates can be visible to other visitors; choose a public pickup area rather than a private home address.

Cookies and recommendations

Essential cookies support sessions and security. If you allow personalization, searches and book views help recommend genres, authors, and languages in your current browser session. Interest preferences expire after 30 days of inactivity. Choosing essential-only clears saved interests.

Push notifications

Push alerts are optional. Enabling them stores a browser subscription so messages and marketplace updates can reach your device. Disable notifications from your profile or browser settings.

Contact and external services

Map links, WhatsApp, browser push services, and externally loaded fonts or icons use third-party services. Their handling of data is separate from this marketplace. Contact support to ask about your stored information or request an account change.
